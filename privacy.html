Privacy Notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This notice explains what personal information Shadwell Dragons collects through this website, why we collect it and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

Shadwell Dragons is a dragon boat racing club based at the Docklands Sailing and Watersports Centre, 235A Westferry Road, London E14 3QS. We are responsible for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us about it at info@shadwelldragons.com.

What we collect

When you use our forms, we collect:

  • Taster session booking: your name, email address, phone number, where you heard about us and any message you add.
  • Contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Corporate enquiries: your name, company, email address, phone number, preferred package, team size and message.
  • Sponsorship enquiries: your name, company, email address, sponsorship level and message.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your taster session.
  • To send you a confirmation email when you contact us.
  • To run the club, for example if you go on to become a member.
  • To protect our forms from spam.
  • To understand how our website is used, so we can improve it.

We rely on our legitimate interests in responding to the enquiries you send us and in running the club. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

We use trusted service providers to host this website, store enquiries, send emails and count visits.

Cookies and storage

We do not set cookies. Your browser temporarily remembers small details during your visit, such as whether you have already seen our donation pop-up. These are cleared when you close your browser. The Google map on our home page and the security check on our forms may set their own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and to run the club, and we regularly delete old enquiries.

Under 18s

Paddlers under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent to join. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. To do this, email info@shadwelldragons.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
