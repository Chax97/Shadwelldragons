Pricing FAQ

When do I start paying?

If you would like to continue after your free taster session, you can join the club by paying before the next session.

How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer or card payment.

Do I get a discount if I join later in the season?

No. Membership is a fixed price for the full season. To keep things fair and consistent, membership covers the season as a whole, no matter when someone joins. Training continues until the centre closes for winter, which depends on water temperature.

What does my membership include?

Your membership covers:

  • All scheduled training sessions throughout the season
  • Use of club equipment during training
  • Coaching and support from our volunteer team
  • Participation in club events and activities
  • Insurance cover through the club's governing body where this applies
When does the season run?

The season runs from spring until the centre closes for winter. The exact end date depends on water temperature and safety conditions. Last year, the centre remained open until October.

Can I pay per session instead of buying a membership?

Yes. We offer a Pay As You Go option at £15 per session, however it does not offer good value if you intend to attend more than 8 sessions that season.

PAYG is suitable for people who want to try a few sessions before committing or who can only attend occasionally. Most regular paddlers find that full membership offers better value across the season.

What about competition fees?

Race entry fees are separate and typically £15-40 per race depending on the event. Members get priority entry.

If I cannot attend every session, is membership still worth it?

Yes. Membership is priced to remain good value even if you cannot attend every session. Many members balance work, family and other commitments, and the structure is designed to be flexible.

Can I pause or transfer my membership?

No. Memberships cannot be paused and cannot be transferred. This keeps the system fair and manageable for all members and volunteers.

What happens if the centre closes early due to weather?

Safety always comes first. If the centre closes earlier than expected because of water temperature or weather conditions, we will communicate this clearly. Membership pricing remains fixed, as it covers the full season's operations and costs.
