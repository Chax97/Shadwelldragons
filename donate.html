Support the Mikaeel Foundation

The Mikaeel Foundation is our charity partner. It was set up in memory of baby Mikaeel, with a mission to keep his name alive by helping people all over the world have a better quality of life, and saving lives where possible.

"Out of darkness comes light. Out of grief comes hope."Mikaeel Foundation

How the Foundation Helps

🤍
Mothers and Families

Support for mothers through pregnancy, miscarriage, stillbirth and child loss.

🏥
Medical Aid

Organising donor drives and providing medical aid, to help save lives where possible.

🌍
Emergency Relief

Assistance for those affected by calamities and natural disasters.

📚
Education

Helping children get the education they deserve.
