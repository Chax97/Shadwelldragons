Support the Mikaeel Foundation

The Mikaeel Foundation is our charity partner. It was founded by Liza Chowdhury, our Women's Team Manager, in memory of her son Mikaeel. Today it helps some of the poorest families, orphans and children in the world, from food and emergency aid to education and a way to earn a living.

"Out of darkness comes light. Out of grief comes hope."Mikaeel Foundation

What Donations Have Achieved

Flood Relief in Bangladesh

Aid for 150 households in Rajnagar, Sunamganj after flooding, including rice, lentils, cooking oil, baby formula, medical saline and hygiene products.

Clothes for Villagers

Cotton sarees and lungis for the most destitute villagers of Ilaigonj, Islampur, in time for Eid-ul-Adha.

A Living for Street Children

Three sewing machines for Chayatal, a refuge for street children in Dhaka, so girls can earn an income instead of begging.

Orphans in Kenya

Support for Heritage of Faith Orphanage and School, home to 120 orphans aged 3 to 18, helping pay for meals, teachers and uniforms.

Education in Burundi

Helping Nissi, a girl with a physical disability, stay in school by covering tuition, books, meals and uniforms.

Children in Gaza

An Eid party with gifts and treats for over 100 children displaced by the war and living in refugee camps.
