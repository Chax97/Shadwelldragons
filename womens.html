Women's Crew

Strong Women, Strong Strokes

The Shadwell Dragons women's crew is a powerhouse of paddlers - friends, fighters, and finishers. From first-time taster sessions to BDA national championships, our women train, race, and celebrate together. Whether you're chasing podiums or just looking for a brilliant midweek workout, you'll find your seat in our boat.

Training Sessions

Our women's crew trains at the Docklands Sailing and Watersports Centre. New paddlers are welcome at our Saturday morning session.

  • Saturday: 10:00am - 12:00pm - Ladies session

Book a free Saturday taster to give it a go - no kit or experience needed.

Get Started

Never paddled before? No problem. Here's how to join the women's crew:

  • Book a free taster session - no kit or experience needed
  • Try a few sessions to find your rhythm
  • Sign up as a member and join us at races and socials
  • Bring a friend - we love welcoming new paddlers together
